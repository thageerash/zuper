App design · Rules for engineering

What each state means, and what it allows.

One vocabulary across Jobs, camera, phase checks, Uploads and Finish. Green appears only when the server has confirmed a photo.

PHOTO STATEMEANSALLOWS
○ MissingRequired, not taken yetBlocks phase complete
✓ CapturedSaved on this phone. Counts toward the phasePhase can complete
● Waiting for signalCaptured, queued, no connectionNever blocks; survives app close
↑ UploadingTransfer in progress—
✓ UploadedServer confirmed receipt. The only greenOffice can see it
! FailedUpload failed; still on this phoneAuto-retries; Retry button
◌ Needs retakeOffice or crew rejected it; original keptRe-opens the phase
✦ AI passed / flaggedAdvice on a captured photoNever blocks
Phase complete
All required photos captured or waived by the PM, and the phase check run. Uploading is not a condition. Reporting an issue never completes or resets a phase; the crew returns to the same phase with its count intact.
Issue ownership
Each issue shows ID, status and next owner: "Awaiting Sean" or "Back to crew". Approval needed to continue (extra work) is marked "Blocks this step"; anything else is "For the record".
Finish job: four separate facts
Work complete (all phases done) · Job closed (closeout accepted on the phone) · Photos uploaded (x of y confirmed) · Office review (pending until Sean opens it). Closing never discards or overstates pending uploads.
Engineering handoff checklist
01Every photo is saved on the phone before it counts as captured.
02Reference photos never count as new evidence.
03A phase is complete when every required photo is captured (or waived by the PM). Upload is not required to move on.
04Queued uploads survive app close, restart and lost signal. Sign-out is blocked while any remain.
05Upload counts are photos, everywhere (badge, tiles, Uploads).
06Every issue shows its ID, status and who acts next (crew or office).
07Work complete, job closed, photos uploaded and office review are four separate facts on screen.
08Retake keeps the original; failed uploads retry; an interrupted closeout resumes where it stopped.
