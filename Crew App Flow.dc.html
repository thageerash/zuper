The crew app: ten screens, built into the Zuper job Ravi already opens every morning.

Based on five screenshots of the current Zuper mobile app. The crew already clocks in, opens a job and taps "Update status" through the day. Photo proof should ride on those habits instead of living in a separate tool.

What the current app gives us

Read from the screenshots. Patterns to reuse on the left, gaps for photo proof on the right.
Screen
Reuse
Gap for photo proof
Home
Greeting, work hours with Start, quick actions, status tiles, job cards with a status pill and priority.
Job cards say nothing about photos. No sign that anything is waiting to upload.
Job detail
Job header with ID and status, Navigate / Add note / New, tabs (Detail, Associated, Status, Notes, Messages), Clock in, orange "Update status" bar.
No place for required photos. Status changes don't check whether evidence exists.
Notes
Free-text note with image, video and file attachments, author and timestamp.
This is where photos live today. Timestamp belongs to the note, not the photo. No phase, area, location or upload state per image.
Maps · Invoice
Out of scope. Kept as they are.
—
Approach 1
Add a Photos tab to the job
Sits next to Detail and Notes. Holds the shot list for the job type. Notes stay for free-form comments.
Approach 2
Tie phases to "Update status"
Moving the job from Arrived to Tear-off is the natural checkpoint. That's where the before-set check appears.
Approach 3
Upload status in the top bar
Next to the bell and chat icons. Always visible, opens the upload queue.

States each screen must handle

These go into wireframes alongside the happy path.
No signal
M1 chip, M4 banner, M8 explains. Everything except upload still works.
Upload failed
M8 row shows reason and Retry. Photo stays on device.
AI wrong or unsure
M5 Change opens the shot list. Unsure → "Check category" on M3.
AI unavailable
M5 uses the shot picked on M3. No quality check, no block.
No location
M5 says "Location not found". Photo still saved.
Camera denied · storage low
M4 shows one-tap fix before the shot, not after.
