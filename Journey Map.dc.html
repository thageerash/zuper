Three moments that decide whether proof exists

Stage 03 · Tear-off
The before set has a deadline.

Once shingles come off, original damage and pre-existing condition are gone. Every dispute and claim depends on photos taken in the first hour on site.

Stage 04 · Deck inspection
A photo is needed now, with no signal.

Rotten decking triggers a change order. The PM needs the photo to get customer approval while the deck is still open.

Stage 08 · Weeks later
Finding it and trusting it.

The office needs one specific photo, fast, with a capture time and location an adjuster or customer will accept.

Personas

Ravi Menon
Crew lead
"No signal on site, so I upload at night. Half never make it."
Context
Outdoors, bright sun, gloves, one hand free, often on a slope. Ten-hour days. Reports progress, issues, change orders and photos to the office.
Needs
Know which shot is next. Know it's saved. Know the office has it. Never redo work.
Success
Leaves site with nothing left to upload or remember.
Sean Doyle
Project manager
"A customer says we broke her gutter. The before photo is on someone's phone. Maybe."
Context
Runs several jobs at once. Days are calls, texts and site visits. Owns customer updates, change orders, closeout and disputes.
Needs
See what's missing before the crew leaves. Find a specific photo during a phone call. Send it to a customer.
Success
Answers "do we have proof of X?" in under a minute.
Priya Shah
Insurance claims
"The insurer rejected our photos. No date, no location, no proof."
Context
Assembles evidence for adjusters. Adjusters expect timestamps, wide and close-up shots, damage patterns, and soft-metal surfaces like gutters as corroboration.
Needs
Original capture time and location kept intact. An organized, labeled set instead of IMG_4837.jpg.
Success
A package an adjuster accepts the first time.

Assumptions to validate

Crews use personal phones, so job photos mix with personal ones.
More than one person on site takes photos, including subcontractors.
The PM does the pre-job inspection, not a separate sales rep.
Insurance work is a large share of Halden's jobs.
Photos lose metadata because they're shared through messaging apps.
