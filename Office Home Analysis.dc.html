What each home shows, and what it leaves out

From Office Inbox v2 we keep the shape: a greeting with one status line, one “needs you now” card on top, lists below, a narrow side column. We drop what doesn’t serve the Problem Statement: AI-picked cards, AI search, customer messages inbox, reports.

SEAN · PROJECT MANAGER · BUILT v2Needs: see gaps before the crew leaves; find one photo during a dispute call. Metric: zero loss.
  1. Greeting + status line. “1 photo at risk · 3 jobs on site”. He reads it between sites.
  2. Needs you now. Only when a spot is open with a photo missing. Primary: Call Ravi. Hidden otherwise, so it never becomes wallpaper.
  3. On site now. Each job: crew, since when, last heard, photos in office in words (“9 of 17 in office”).
  4. Finished today. Shows what’s left: missing with reason, still on phone.
  5. Side · Not in office after check-out. The zero-loss watch, with when the 4 h alert fires.
  6. Side · Recently shared. Links sent to customers and whether opened. Helps when the same customer calls back.
Left out: claims (Priya’s job; Sean sees claim status on the Job page), schedule and tomorrow’s jobs (out of scope), photo counts per crew, charts.
PRIYA · INSURANCE CLAIMS · BUILTNeeds: a labeled set with time and location intact that an adjuster accepts the first time. Metric: accepted claims.
  1. Greeting + status line. “1 ready to send · 1 waiting for an answer”.
  2. Ready to send. Finished jobs with every photo in the office, each with what an adjuster will ask about. Primary on the top one: Check the set.
  3. Adjuster asked for more. Only when it happens; what they asked and when. Most urgent for her metric.
  4. Waiting for an answer. Sent packages, days since sent, quick Accepted / Asked for more.
  5. Side · Waiting on photos. Finished jobs she can’t send yet (#424, 3 on Dana’s phone). Read only; Sean follows up.
  6. Side · First-time acceptance. One number for the month, because it is the metric she is judged on.
Left out: live jobs and alerts (Sean’s), Call Ravi, payment or claim amounts (insurer systems, out of scope).
