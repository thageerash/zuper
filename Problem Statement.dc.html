Halden Roofing · Photo documentation · Problem statement v1
← Journey map

Halden's crews take plenty of photos. Few of them work as evidence.

Photos are captured without a plan, stored on personal phones, uploaded unreliably, and stripped of the context that proves when and where they were taken. When a customer disputes the work or an insurer asks for proof, the office can't find the right photo or show it can be trusted.

Three underlying failures

01 · Capture
Incomplete capture

Nothing tells the crew which photos a job needs, or when. The shots that matter most (arrival condition, roof before tear-off, exposed decking) have short windows that close as work progresses. Once one is missed, it's gone.

02 · Custody
Unreliable custody

With no signal on site, photos sit on personal phones and get uploaded in batches at night. Uploads fail silently. The crew can't tell what the office actually received.

03 · Proof
Unprovable context

Photos lose their capture time and location when shared through messaging apps. Without a date and location, insurers reject them and they don't settle disputes.

How might we

How might we help a roofing crew capture the right photos at the right moment, even with no signal, so the office can find any photo in seconds and share it with its capture time and location still provable?
