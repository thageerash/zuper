My approach

Three habits I kept from the first prompt to the last.
01
Start from the problem, not the screen Before drawing anything, I asked who loses photos, where, and why. That became one problem in three parts: capture, custody and proof. In practice · Every flow was checked against those three words before it was built.
02
Design the hard case first On a roof, no signal is a normal day. So offline is part of the main flow, not an error screen. In practice · Ravi can check out with photos still on his phone, and the app never says “sent” too early.
03
AI drafts, I decide Claude explored options and built the prototypes. I reviewed each answer and kept, changed or rejected it. In practice · I changed the AI’s answer at 20 turning points, and every screen was audited for hierarchy, contrast and counts that add up.
